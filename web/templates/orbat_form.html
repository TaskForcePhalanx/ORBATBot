The format 
Squad lines start at the left margin, slots are indented:

1-1 Alpha  | right, unit:TFP, radio:343 CHN:3
  Squad Leader
  Rifleman

Everything after the | belongs to the squad, separated by commas:

  | left, unit:2nd USC, radio:343 CHN:3

Squad options
  left / right   which column of the board the squad sits in
  unit:TAG       the unit the whole squad belongs to, e.g. unit:TFP
  radio:...      the channel the squad talks on internally
  nocount        leave the squad out of the open/filled counts

A line starting with # is a comment.
A leading number ("1. Rifleman") is stripped.
