The longest kills, to be checked

The farthest infantry kills, as they count towards the longest and average kill distance — including every player’s own longest, which is the number next to their name on the dashboard. Tick a kill that is not believable — a weapon OCAP got wrong, a shot that cannot have happened — and save: it still counts as a kill, but its distance leaves the statistics, it moves to *Struck kills* below, and the next-farthest kill takes its place. Untick a struck kill there to put it back. Kills from a vehicle are not listed (a vehicle’s weapons reach that far), and mortars and other indirect fire, pistols beyond 300 m, crew of a destroyed vehicle and on-foot kills beyond 1500 m are left out already.
