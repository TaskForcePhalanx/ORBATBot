{# The gesture is the one Arma's own map uses, so it is what people try first — but nothing on the page would otherwise say it is here. #}

Draw a line by dragging: with the Line or Area tool, or by holding Ctrl from any tool. Hold Shift while you drag for a straight segment — let go of it and freehand carries on, hold it again for the next corner. Esc throws the stroke away. Rectangles and ellipses are dragged corner to corner — Shift makes a square or a circle — and resized or turned by their handles once selected. The AO tool greys out the map and clears each area you draw; on a campaign map, every AO drawn becomes one of the campaign's AOs when you save.

    Keys

    • V select · U unit · M marker · L line · A area · R rectangle · E ellipse · O AO · T text
    • Line and area: drag to draw, Shift for a straight segment, Esc cancels
    • Rectangle and ellipse: drag corner to corner, Shift for a square or circle; drag a corner handle to resize, the round one to turn (Shift snaps to 15°)
    • Del removes the selection · Ctrl+Z undoes · Ctrl+S saves
    • Scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan
