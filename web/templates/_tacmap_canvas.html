    Keys and gestures

    Lines work as on Arma's map: hold Ctrl and click each corner, let go of Ctrl to finish — or hold Ctrl and drag for freehand. The Line and Area tools do the same without Ctrl (double-click or Enter to finish). Shift keeps a segment straight and snaps it to 15°; Esc throws it away. Rectangles and ellipses are dragged corner to corner — Shift makes a square or a circle. The AO tool greys out the map and clears each area you draw; on a campaign map, every AO drawn becomes one of the campaign's AOs when you save.

    • V select · U unit · M marker · L line · A area · R rectangle · E ellipse · O AO · T text · D measure · P phase line
    • Shift+click adds to the selection, Shift+drag on empty ground selects by box, Ctrl+A selects everything shown · Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V copy and paste, even into another map · Ctrl+D duplicates
    • Selected line or area: drag a corner to move it, drag a segment's middle dot to add one, Alt+click or right-click a corner to delete it
    • Line and area: Ctrl+click corners (let go of Ctrl to finish) or Ctrl+drag freehand; Shift straight and 15° snap, Enter or double-click finishes, Esc cancels
    • Rectangle and ellipse: drag corner to corner, Shift for a square or circle; drag a corner handle to resize, the round one to turn (Shift snaps to 15°)
    • Del removes the selection · Ctrl+Z undoes · Ctrl+S saves
    • Scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan
